Between Water and Fire (Tra Acqua e Fuoco)

Weirdos Series – Vol. 4

Weirdos #4

Between Water
and Fire

Weirdos Series Vol. 4 304 pages Italian 2 August 2025

A soul that lost its own heart far too soon. Scarred and disoriented, yet so big-hearted and caring that he doesn't realise he's the very cornerstone of his family. This is Alec, a young man who lost the love of his life far too soon, who had happiness pulled out from under his feet, who had hope stolen from him.

A pure, innocent and fragile heart, yet at the same time tenacious and determined. This is Hope, a simple and generous girl searching for true love — the kind that makes you burn with passion, that takes your breath away, that makes you dream.

The Yin and the Yang, water and fire, two sides of the same coin in constant transformation, complementary, where each is essential to the other's change and harmony.

A story of courage and hope, which teaches us that even the most raging fire, when the water comes, can only draw back its flames to make room for life and rebirth.
