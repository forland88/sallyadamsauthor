Weirdos Series

The novels in the series

Each volume stands alone, but the characters intertwine — I recommend reading them in order.

Weirdos #1

Between Ice
and Clouds

Weirdos Series · Vol. 1 · 298 pp. · June 2022

Between Ice and Clouds (Tra Ghiaccio e Nuvole)

Viky & Damon

Twelve months have passed since her ex-boyfriend beat her within an inch of her life and, even though she woke from the coma, Viky seems trapped in the terrible darkness of that moment. Damon is surly and impenetrable. He has learned that you can't trust anyone — let alone that intruder who makes his stomach twist in a strange way.

A story of deep friendship and unexpected love. A story that teaches us that even the thickest ice can melt, and that clouds don't always bring rain.

Weirdos #2

Between Light
and Darkness

Weirdos Series · Vol. 2 · 328 pp. · April 2023

Between Light and Darkness (Tra Luce e Tenebre)

Paul & Gin

For Paul, making it in the music world means everything. He can't do anything but play, write melodies and sing. But when the long-awaited chance finally comes, life mocks him once more and his whole world seems to crumble. For Gin, simplicity and family are everything. She's the baby of the house, the little sister loved and pampered by her brothers.

The combination of Paul and Gin. A story that teaches us that, despite everything, each of us can step out of the darkness when, little by little, we let ourselves be wrapped in the light.

Strangers to lovers Hurt-comfort Found family

Weirdos #3

Between Sun
and Storm

Weirdos Series · Vol. 3 · 296 pp. · October 2024

Between Sun and Storm (Tra Sole e Tempesta)

Jackie (JJ) & Angel

Strong, tenacious and determined, yet at the same time fragile, lost and alone. That's how Jackie feels — constantly and relentlessly judged, criticised… ordered around since childhood. Without realising it, Angel and JJ have far more in common than their supposed incompatibility keeps trying to make them believe. They're like a puzzle, where each is the other's missing piece.

«You're my sun, you know? You always light up everything with your glow… even the darkest corners.»

Enemies to lovers Romantic suspense Spicy

Weirdos #4

Between Water
and Fire

Weirdos Series · Vol. 4 · 304 pp. · August 2025

Between Water and Fire (Tra Acqua e Fuoco)

Alec & Hope

A soul that lost its own heart far too soon. Scarred and disoriented, yet so big-hearted and caring that he doesn't realise he's the very cornerstone of his family. A pure, innocent and fragile heart, yet at the same time tenacious and determined. This is Hope, a simple and generous girl, searching for true love.

The Yin and the Yang, water and fire, two sides of the same coin in constant transformation, complementary.
