Between Sun and Storm (Tra Sole e Tempesta)

Weirdos Series – Vol. 3

Weirdos #3

Between Sun
and Storm

Weirdos Series Vol. 3 296 pages Italian 19 October 2024

Strong, tenacious and determined, yet at the same time fragile, lost and alone. That's how Jackie feels — constantly and relentlessly judged, criticised… ordered around since childhood.

Loving, selfless and protective, yet at the same time disoriented, different and wrong. That's how Angel feels — for years intimately blamed and betrayed by the man who should have loved him no matter what, simply because he followed his heart.

Without realising it, Angel and JJ have far more in common than their supposed incompatibility keeps trying to make them believe. They're like a puzzle, where each is the other's missing piece. They'll get to know each other, understand each other, and learn that maybe it's the very people who judge them who are in the wrong — blind to the truth, driven only by their own ignorance.

A story of courage; a story that teaches us that after the storm there is always the sun, whose rays find their way through the clouds, brightening the dark and reaching ever deeper.

Excerpt

«You're my sun, you know? You always light up everything with your glow… even the darkest corners.»
«And you're my storm… You walked into my life and swept everything away.»
