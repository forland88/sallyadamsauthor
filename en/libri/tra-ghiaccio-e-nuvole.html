Between Ice and Clouds (Tra Ghiaccio e Nuvole)

Weirdos Series – Vol. 1

Weirdos #1

Between Ice
and Clouds

Weirdos Series Vol. 1 298 pages Italian 18 June 2022

Twelve months have passed since her ex-boyfriend beat her within an inch of her life and, even though she woke from the coma, Viky seems trapped in the terrible darkness of that moment.

Damon is surly and impenetrable. He has learned that you can't trust anyone — let alone that intruder who makes his stomach twist in a strange way.

And what if, just when life finally seems to be going the right way, the past came knocking at their door and tried to sweep everything away?

A story of deep friendship and unexpected love. A story that teaches us that even the thickest ice can melt, and that clouds don't always bring rain.

Excerpt

«I wanted his hands on me, his mouth, his body. I wanted to watch his eyes dance across my skin. I wanted him to see all my scars, outside and in, and to heal my body and my soul as no one ever had before.
I wanted his heart. I wanted him.»
