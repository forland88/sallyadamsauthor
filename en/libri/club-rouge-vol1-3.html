Club Rouge

Vol. 1–3
Collection

Club Rouge Collection Vol. 1 + 2 + 3 Italian

Three novellas, three stories, one club where everything is allowed except ignoring desire.

The collection brings together the first three novellas of The Club Rouge Novellas: Look at Me (Guardami), Spoil Me (Viziami) and Touch Me (Toccami).

Three different couples, three ways of loving and wanting each other. Three short, white-hot stories you'll read in one sitting, tied together by a single setting: Club Rouge, where the night lasts forever and rules exist only to be broken.

For those who want it all at once — because stopping after the first story is impossible.

Dark romance Forbidden desire Spicy Club setting Collection
