Serve Me

Club Rouge #4 Novella Italian

At Club Rouge there are unwritten rules. The first: don't mix business with pleasure. The second: don't fall in love. The third is the one no one ever manages to keep.

A story of forbidden attraction, of boundaries that shift one evening after another, of desires that grow harder and harder to ignore. Between stepchildren and life partners, between a sense of duty and unbridled passion.

Because sometimes serving is the most powerful way of commanding.
