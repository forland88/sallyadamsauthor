Between Light and Darkness (Tra Luce e Tenebre)

Weirdos Series – Vol. 2

Weirdos #2

Between Light
and Darkness

Weirdos Series Vol. 2 328 pages Italian 5 April 2023

For Paul, making it in the music world means everything. He can't do anything but play, write melodies and sing. She has always been there for him and, even though life loves to slap him in the face, music has always saved him. But when the long-awaited chance finally comes, life mocks him once more and his whole world seems to crumble.

But what if, this time, music isn't the only thing that pulls him out of the abyss?

For Gin, simplicity and family are everything. She's the baby of the house, the little sister loved and pampered by her brothers. She has a job she enjoys, but none of it seems to be enough anymore once she meets Paul.

Drawn to his deep, dark eyes and to everything behind that loose-limbed walk and that tall mohawk, will she manage to save him from his shadows and light up his darkness?

The combination of Paul and Gin. A story that teaches us that, despite everything, each of us can step out of the darkness when, little by little, we let ourselves be wrapped in the light.
