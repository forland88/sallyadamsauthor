Novellas

Five short, white-hot novellas set in a single club where the night never ends and rules exist only to be broken. A darker, more sensual side of Sally Adams' writing.

The novellas

Each novella stands alone and can be read independently. Together, they build the unique atmosphere of Club Rouge.

Club Rouge #1

Look at Me

Club Rouge · Novella #1

Look at Me (Guardami)

The lights are low, the music is muffled. You come to Club Rouge to be seen — or to disappear. This is the story of someone who can't quite manage either.

Because sometimes asking «look at me» is the bravest thing in the world.

Dark romance Forbidden desire Spicy Club setting

Club Rouge #2

Spoil Me

Club Rouge · Novella #2

Spoil Me (Viziami)

At Club Rouge you learn one rule fast: everything has a price. But when the price to pay is your own heart, the bill gets complicated.

A story of luxury, excess and that moment when someone stops being a habit and becomes a need. An intense novella where the line between spoiling and being spoiled vanishes in a single night.

Because some desires, once lit, never go out again.

Dark romance Luxury setting Forbidden desire Spicy

Club Rouge #3

Touch Me

Club Rouge · Novella #3

Touch Me (Toccami)

Some touches leave a mark even when the hands are long gone. Some people barely brush against you and change everything, without you even noticing.

At Club Rouge, where every sense is heightened and every boundary is thin, two strangers discover that the most dangerous desire isn't the one of the body — it's the one that nests in the chest and never leaves.

A story written on the skin. A slow-burning novella.

Dark romance Strangers Sensory Spicy Club setting
Book details Amazon
Club Rouge #4

Serve Me

Club Rouge · Novella #4

Serve Me (Servimi)

At Club Rouge there are unwritten rules. The first: don't mix business with pleasure. The second: don't fall in love. The third is the one no one ever manages to keep.

A story of forbidden attraction, of boundaries that shift one evening after another, of desires that grow harder and harder to ignore. Between stepchildren and life partners, between a sense of duty and unbridled passion.

Because sometimes serving is the most powerful way of commanding.

Dark romance Forbidden attraction Step-siblings adjacent Spicy Club setting
Book details Amazon
Club Rouge #5

Bind Me

Club Rouge · Novella #5

Bind Me (Legami)

She is a Shibari model, he is an expert rigger. On the Club Rouge stage their bodies speak a language made of knots, tension and absolute trust.

Una notte, una performance, due anime unite dalla stessa passione per la corda. Chloe e Cameron scoprono che ciò che li lega davanti al pubblico potrebbe diventare qualcosa di molto più profondo… o restare soltanto il ricordo di una sera.

Because some ties never come undone when the curtain falls.

Dark romance Shibari Dom/Sub Spicy Club setting
