Hi, I'm Sally Adams

Born in 1990, Sally Adams grew up in a small town near Milan. A lifelong book lover, she devours books in just a few days — or even hours when she really gets going. She reads a bit everywhere, whenever she gets the chance; the moment she has a free minute you'll find her with her nose buried in yet another book, devouring yet another story.

She doesn't stick to a single genre, ranging from crime to literary novels, from thrillers to fantasy, from the paranormal to serial-killer biographies.

«Between Ice and Clouds (Tra ghiaccio e nuvole)» is her first novel. A story born many years ago that, were it not for her best friend, she would never have had the courage to publish. Now, instead, it's just waiting to be read.

After the success of her first novel, Sally kept writing with the same passion and intensity that marks every one of her stories: deep characters, genuine emotions and loves that leave a mark. The Weirdos Series was born this way — from one story calling for another, from couples that deserved their own voice.

With The Club Rouge Novellas she set out to explore a darker, more sensual side of romance fiction, creating short but intense stories set in an exclusive club where everything is allowed except ignoring desire.
