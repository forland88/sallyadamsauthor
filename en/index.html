about

Hi, I'm Sally

Born in 1990 near Milan, I devour books in a matter of days — when a story really grips me, even in a few hours. Mystery, thriller, fantasy, paranormal, true crime: if there's a good story, I'll read it.

Between Ice and Clouds (Tra ghiaccio e nuvole) is my debut novel, a story conceived years ago that I would never have published without the encouragement of my best friend. Now I can't stop writing.
